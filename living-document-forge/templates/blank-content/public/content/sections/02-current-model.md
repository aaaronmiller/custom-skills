## Describe the present understanding

Replace this guidance with the project’s current model. Separate established facts from hypotheses, implementation candidates, examples, open questions, and known risks.

Explain how major parts relate. Use stable terms consistently and add diagrams or resources only when they clarify mechanism or lineage.

## Preserve disagreement

Do not force uncertain material into authoritative prose. Attach an objection, create a proposal, or record an experiment. A living document should show where understanding is strong and where it still bends under pressure.
