## Record decisions as objects

Separate accepted direction, rejected alternatives, deferred possibilities, and experiments. A proposal should be narrow enough to approve or reject without deciding an entire worldview at once.

## Make the next revision obvious

Name the immediate work, its dependency, and the evidence required to close it. Link each action to the section or proposal it changes.

The dashboard focus should reflect the most consequential unresolved issue, not merely the newest note.
