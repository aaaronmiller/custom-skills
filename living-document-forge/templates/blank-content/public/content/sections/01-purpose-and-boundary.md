## State the job of the document

Replace this neutral guidance with a direct account of the problem the document governs. Name the practical outcome it should improve: a decision, implementation, research program, operating procedure, or shared model.

Record the intended audience and the authority of the document. A design concept may guide exploration without acting as a final specification. A specification may constrain implementation without claiming to settle the underlying research.

## Define the boundary

List adjacent concerns that matter but do not belong in this document. A clear boundary prevents every useful idea from becoming another section and protects the document from turning into an unsearchable attic.

The first revision should leave a reader able to answer: **Why does this exist, who uses it, and what does it not control?**
