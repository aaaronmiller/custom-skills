# Steering contract (the owner's rules, 2026-10-14)

These came from the owner while the first version was being built. They outrank anything else in this skill.

1. **Group, don't itemize.** A per-file punch list costs the owner as much time as sorting the files themselves. Group items into similar types and purposes and ask for decisions per group.
2. **Almost everything is kept on purpose.** "Virtually everything is intended for storage - that's why it's there." Remove only what is obviously superseded by a newer version, a duplicate, or a temp file that was never meant for long-term storage.
3. **Organize the rest by type and purpose.** Use datestamps to group related files into projects.
4. **Installers get three homes:** already installed, ready to install, and for systems other than this machine.
5. **Use what is known, then look closer.** Reuse earlier descriptions, and examine content (especially markdown and other text documents) where the name is not enough.
6. **Minimize the owner's effort.** The deliverable should not depend on the owner reviewing every item.
7. **Work out intent for every item:** why is it here, why was it downloaded, what was the owner trying to do with it, and what would they want done.
8. **"Unsure" is a valid answer.** Label items you cannot work out; that gives the owner a smaller set to handle personally.
9. **Decisions are made by a model reasoning, not by a filter or script.** Information can be gathered programmatically; the judgement must come from an LLM.
10. **Cover every folder**, not just Downloads: Desktop, Documents, Pictures/Music/Videos, the home folder, other drives, temp/caches.
11. **Improve as you go** through periodic meta-analysis of the results, and fold the improvements back into the method.
12. **Codify the result as a reusable skill** for similar tasks, generic in method but carrying owner-specific concepts.
13. **Leave security tooling alone.** Keymakers, cracked installers, MDM tools, credential files and the owner's security settings are intentional; never isolate, move or comment on them.
