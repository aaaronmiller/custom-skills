## Things Learned From Prior Hunts

- **Wellfound pagination:** `?page=N` works server-side without JS or login — 4 pages yielded 66 jobs (SSR, no browser needed). Browse daemon timed out on p3 during live attempt — requests+BeautifulSoup was the working bypass. Salary lines (`$MBk–$UBk`) are reliable anchors; backwards scan finds role/company/size.
- **Built In:** `curl + regex` on `data-id="job-card"` / `company-title` / `job-card-title` is more reliable than API. JSON-LD lacks company/salary. Seattle URL filter is weak — many national/remote jobs leak in.
- **GeekWire 200:** Full page requires JS to render ranking widget after CF challenge. Direct WAF bypass via iPhone UA got HTML shell but no table — actual data lives behind JS widget. Articles (Q1 March + Q2 June) gave 33 ranked companies with domains/sectors. Full 200 needs Hunt 3 browser bypass with CF solve.
- **General:** No more than ~26 true 2–50 small startups per Wellfound page — the small-startup tail is real but thin past p5.
