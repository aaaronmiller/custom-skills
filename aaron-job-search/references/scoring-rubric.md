# Scoring Rubric — 6-Factor Job Fit

Each job scored 1–10 per factor. Composite = Likelihood×1.5 + Income×1.3 + Growth + Stability + Flexibility + SkillLeverage. Max 68. Tie-break by Likelihood.

## 1. Likelihood of Success (×1.5)

| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 10 | Exact stack match (TS/Claude API/MCP/Bun/Hono), 0–3 YOE ask, fast-moving startup, 1-click apply |
| 8 | Strong match, 1–2 stretch skills learnable in days, Seattle small co |
| 6 | Partial match, 3–5 YOE ask but portfolio covers it, moderate competition |
| 4 | Major gap (e.g., 7+ YOE, niche domain like biotech), high competition |
| 2 | Credential gate (PhD, PE license) or 10+ YOE |

Adjust +1 if hiring velocity high (posted <7d), -1 if >200 applicants noted.

## 2. Income (×1.3) — Seattle-adjusted

Score from band. If Wellfound/Levels/Glassdoor missing → [INFERRED] via role × stage.

| Score | Band |
|-------|------|
| 10 | 180K+ total comp |
| 8 | 140–180K |
| 6 | 110–140K |
| 4 | 80–110K |
| 2 | <80K |

Equity counts: seed equity 0.05–0.2% noted separately.

## 3. Growth Potential

Learning, promotion path, skill compounding. 10 = senior track, mentorship, greenfield ownership. 2 = dead-end, maintenance only.

## 4. Stability

Funding stage, runway, Glassdoor, reputation. 10 = Series B+ / profitable, 6+ months runway signal. 4 = pre-seed no revenue. Check Crunchbase if available.

## 5. Flexibility / Location

10 = remote or Seattle proper, async, no on-call. 6 = hybrid Eastside. 2 = 5-day on-site with commute + heavy on-call.

## 6. Skill Leverage (Aaron's differentiator)

10 = cross-domain edge is the moat (AI agent orchestration + WSL2/systems + hardware repair + semiconductor thesis + knowledge systems). 6 = generic SWE where Aaron is commodity. 2 = role ignores every strength.

## Likelihood Letter Grade (for table)

S ≥9, A 7–8, B 5–6, C 3–4, D ≤2 — with one-line reason referencing gap or match.
