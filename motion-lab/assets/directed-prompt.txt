GOAL: what the viewer should feel or do, in one sentence
OUTPUT: one self-contained HTML file (or Remotion, HyperFrames), W x H
TIME: seconds x fps = total frames. Loop: yes or no. BPM if the cuts follow a beat
LOOK: palette in hex, one accent, typeface + weight + size + tracking
MOTION: easing as cubic-bezier, springs as damping ratio, durations in frames
SHOTS: frame ranges, one hero move per shot, how each shot hands off (cut on the beat, match cut, mask wipe)
BANNED: the defaults you never want to see
QA: stills at named frames, a contact sheet, fix, then the full render

# Add for 3D
MODEL: units, primitives and sizes, materials with roughness, metalness and clearcoat
CAMERA: field of view, distance, orbit range and easing, push-ins with their curve and time; never parked
LIGHTS: key, fill and rim with color, strength and position; reflections and contact shadows

# Add for anything with an identity
KEEP: what may not change (a character's proportions, a logo's geometry, brand colors), in numbers
